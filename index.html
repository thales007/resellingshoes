About the guy behind this.

I didn't start out as a shoe reseller. I started out as someone who needed extra money and was willing to do whatever it took to find it — electronics, clothes, kitchen appliances, anything that looked like it had value at a yard sale.

The problem was that every item was different. Every sale was its own project. I was spending more time figuring out what I had than building something consistent.

Then I started paying attention to shoes. Goodwill prices. Strong resale value. Easy to ship. Massive market. So I narrowed my focus — stopped buying everything and started buying shoes.

I've done over $100,000 in shoe sales — not hype sneakers, just everyday brands from Goodwill and yard sales.

A few hundred dollars a month can genuinely change a family's financial situation. It creates breathing room. It removes pressure. I know what it has done for mine. That's why this site exists.

— Timothy Hales, ResellingShoes.com
