About

Helping you earn your
first $1,000.

I'm Timothy Hales. ResellingShoes.com exists for one reason: to help part-time resellers earn their first $1,000 flipping everyday shoes — Merrell, Dansko, New Balance, Brooks, Keen — not hype sneakers.

Most reselling content online is built for people chasing Jordans and limited drops. That's a different game with different rules. This site is for the person with a Goodwill nearby, a few hours a week, and a willingness to learn the actual system: what to buy, how to clean and photograph it, how to price it, and how to ship it without losing your margin.

That system is what became The $1,000 Shoe Reselling Method (free) and The Everyday Shoe Flip (the complete playbook). I also built SoleScore, a free tool that scores a shoe's resale potential in seconds, right from the thrift store aisle.
