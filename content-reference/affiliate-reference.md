## STRATEGY NOTES

**"What I use" framing, not "best of."** First-person single-recommendation framing throughout. More credible, harder to replicate, converts better.

**Process articles are buying guides.** Products embedded naturally at the point in the workflow where they're used, not dumped at top/bottom.

**Never publish without confirmed links.** Every product must have a confirmed, live affiliate link before an article goes live.

**Amazon link maintenance.** `amzn.to` short links can break if products are discontinued. Check quarterly.

**Disclosure requirement (FTC).** Every article with affiliate links needs: "This post contains affiliate links. I may earn a commission if you purchase through them at no extra cost to you." Near the top, or in site footer.
